About

Locally run. Built on hands-on repair work.

Genuine Electric Lifts Tech PTE LTD is a Nausori-based service company covering forklifts, material-handling equipment, and the electrical and security systems that keep commercial sites running. The workshop sits in Kasavu, Nausori — about a 14-minute drive from central Nausori — with technicians available for on-site call-outs.

The work spans general mechanical servicing through to electronics repair, alarm and CCTV installation, and industrial electrical maintenance, so most equipment breakdowns can be handled by a single call rather than several different contractors.

Downtime is the real cost when a forklift or scrubber goes down. The focus here is turnaround: assess the fault, repair on the spot or hire out a replacement charger, and get the equipment back on the floor.

How it works

From first call to equipment back on the floor

A straightforward sequence, whether it’s a workshop drop-off or an on-site call-out.

01

Call or email

Describe the equipment and the fault. Most calls get an initial response the same day.

02

Diagnosis

On site or at the Kasavu workshop, the fault is assessed and you’re given a clear picture before any work starts.

03

Repair or hire

The equipment is repaired, or a charger or unit is hired out so your operation doesn’t stop while parts arrive.

04

Handover

Equipment is tested and handed back, with the option of a follow-up check if needed.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you come on-site, or is it workshop drop-off only?
Both. Smaller equipment and electronics are often easiest to bring to the Kasavu workshop, while forklifts, lifts, and installed systems like CCTV are usually serviced on-site. Call ahead and we’ll advise the best option for your situation.
Can I hire a battery charger while mine is being repaired?
Yes — short-term AC/DC battery charger hire is available so your forklift or equipment doesn’t sit idle while the original unit is being fixed.
What areas do you cover?
The workshop is based in Kasavu, Nausori, with regular call-outs across Nausori, Nasinu, Suva, and the wider Rewa Province. If you’re outside this area, get in touch and we can confirm whether a visit is possible.
Do you install new CCTV and access control systems, or only repair them?
Both new installation and ongoing maintenance are covered for alarm systems, CCTV, and biometric access control, for both homes and businesses.
How do I get a quote?
Call 714 9329, email genuineelectriclifts@gmail.com, or use the quote request form below with details of the equipment and the fault, and you’ll be contacted to arrange a look or a call-out.
