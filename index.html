01 — Who we are

Locally run. Built on hands-on repair work.

Genuine Electric Lifts Tech PTE LTD is a Nausori-based service company covering forklifts, material-handling equipment, and the electrical and security systems that keep commercial sites running. The workshop sits in Kasavu, Nausori — about a 14-minute drive from central Nausori — with technicians available for on-site call-outs.

The work covers general mechanical servicing through to electronics repair, alarm and CCTV installation, and industrial electrical maintenance, so most equipment breakdowns can be handled by a single call rather than several different contractors.

Fiji businesses lose real time to equipment sitting idle waiting on parts or a technician. The focus here is turnaround: assess the fault, repair or hire a replacement where needed, and get the equipment back on the floor.
